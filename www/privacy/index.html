Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09/23/2026

This policy covers rusefi.com (including the forum and rusEFI Online), wiki.rusefi.com and shop.rusefi.com.

Who we are: Rusefi LLC · Jersey City, NJ, United States · rusefillc@gmail.com

In short

  • We collect only what we need to deliver your order, answer your questions and run the sites.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • Your card details go directly to the payment provider. We never see your full card number.
  • What you post on the forum or upload to rusEFI Online (tunes, logs) is public.
  • Email us any time to get, correct or delete your data.

What we collect and why

Data Why we use it How long we keep it
Orders (from our store or our eBay listings): name, shipping and billing address, email, phone, items bought To take payment, ship your order, support it and handle warranty claims; tax and accounting records As long as tax law requires (usually up to 7 years)
Unfinished checkout: email address and cart contents To send you one reminder about your unfinished order Until you finish the order or ask us to delete it
Messages you send us by email or the contact form To answer you and provide support As long as needed to resolve your request
Forum and rusEFI Online accounts: username, email, password (stored hashed), IP address, posts, uploaded tunes and logs To run your account and the community. Posts, tunes and logs are public Until you delete your account or ask us to
Donations via PayPal: name, email, amount To record the donation As long as tax law requires
Site usage: pages viewed, clicks, device and browser, approximate location (city, country), where you came from To see which pages help visitors, fix problems and keep the sites secure Analytics data: up to 14 months

You don't have to give us your data, but we can't ship an order without a name and address.

Cookies and analytics

  • Essential cookies keep your cart and checkout working. They can't be switched off.
  • Google Analytics (all our sites) shows us how the sites are used. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Meta Pixel (rusefi.com) measures visits from Facebook and Instagram and lets us show our ads there to people who visited our site. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Where the law requires it, analytics and advertising cookies are set only after you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice using the cookie settings link at the bottom of the page.
  • Our sites don't respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Emails

  • Order emails (confirmation, shipping, tracking) are part of your order.
  • One reminder if you enter your email at checkout but don't finish the order.
  • Marketing emails only if you sign up. Every one has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share data with

Only with services that help us run the business, and only for the purposes above:

  • Squarespace runs our online store.
  • Squarespace Payments and PayPal process payments.
  • USPS and UPS deliver your order. If a parcel goes through customs, the customs declaration (name, address, contents, value) goes to customs.
  • Google provides analytics (including order totals and country, but not names or addresses) and our email.
  • Meta receives Meta Pixel data.
  • eBay and PayPal, if you buy through our eBay listings or donate. Their own privacy policies also apply.

We also share data when the law requires it, for example with tax authorities.

Links to other sites, such as Facebook, YouTube, Discord and GitHub, lead to services with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle your data.

Where your data is stored

We are based in the United States. Our service providers store data in the United States and may also store it in other countries.

Your rights

Email rusefillc@gmail.com to:

  • get a copy of your data;
  • correct it;
  • delete it (we keep only what tax law requires us to keep);
  • stop marketing emails.

We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm it's you, for example by writing from the email used for your order.

Security

We use trusted providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access to data. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

Children

Our sites and products are not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we also post a notice on the site.
