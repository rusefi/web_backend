What rusEFI is

rusEFI is open-source engine management: the firmware on GitHub and the boards built for it. One firmware runs every board: sequential fuel and spark up to 12 cylinders, drive-by-wire, boost and launch control, Lua scripting. Choose your hardware by engine size.

How we work
  • Maximum features per dollar: online tuning, electronic throttle, built-in wideband oxygen-sensor control, CAN and SD logging.
  • Open source since 2012. Firmware, schematics and board files are on GitHub.
  • Software written the way software should be written: continuous integration, unit tests, issue tracking, full history of every change.
  • Docs are on the wiki, help is the community: forum, Discord, Facebook group. One-on-one technical support is sold separately, from $110.
  • Boards ship from New Jersey, USA, usually within 5 business days.

uaEFI

uaEFI - standalone ECU for 1–6 cylinder port-injected gasoline engines. Sequential fuel and spark, wideband controller, knock input and drive-by-wire on one board, fully assembled. From $275.

On the board
  • 6 sequential injector outputs and 6 sequential ignition outputs (smart coils); up to 8 injectors on simpler engines
  • Bosch LSU 4.9 wideband controller, knock sensor input, digital baro sensor, flex-fuel input
  • 2 DC motor drivers, 6 A each: dual electronic throttle, throttle + electronic wastegate, or stepper idle
  • SD card logging, CAN bus, USB
  • Inputs: 2 VR, 3 Hall, 9+2 analog. 5 Molex Mini-Fit Jr. plugs, 68 pins; 100 × 100 mm

You add: MAP sensor (solder-on or external), Bosch LSU 4.9 sensor, harness, igniters for non-smart coils. Tuned with TunerStudio.

Getting started

Every board runs the same firmware, so the path is the same. The full roadmap is on the wiki: Getting Started; Quick Start is the short version for when the ECU is already on the bench.

  1. 1

    Pick a board

    The table above; specs side by side in the ECU comparison. Not sure? Post your engine on the forum or Discord before you order.

  2. 2

    Get the software

    Download the bundle for your board (firmware, rusEFI Console, drivers, TunerStudio definition) and create a TunerStudio project. Boards ship with firmware installed; update it before the first start.

  3. 3

    Wire it

    Power, grounds, sensors, injectors, coils: wiring overview. Plug-and-play boards skip this step.

  4. 4

    Connect and start

    Connect TunerStudio over USB, set the trigger, then follow Get running (or the plug-and-play version).

  5. 5

    Tune, log, share

    Get tuning; record proper logs and post tune and logs on rusEFI Online when you ask for help.

FAQ

What does open source mean for me?

The firmware is open source for every board. Hardware files (schematics, PCB, gerbers) are published for uaEFI and several other boards on GitHub. You can see exactly what your ECU does, and the price includes no license fee.

Do I need a tuner?

Many owners tune themselves with the wiki and community help. One-on-one technical support from rusEFI is a separate item in the store, from $110.

What software do I use?

TunerStudio (third-party, Windows/Mac/Linux) over USB. rusEFI firmware is free, open source and updated on GitHub.

Can I run a street car on it?

rusEFI ECUs are sold for competition use. They do not control emissions equipment and are not approved for road use. Check your local rules.

Where do you ship from, and how long does it take?

From New Jersey, USA. In-stock orders usually ship within 5 business days. USPS (cheaper) or UPS (faster, better tracking) - you pick at checkout.
